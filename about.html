About Auxora Nanolabs

About Auxora Nanolabs

Auxora Nanolabs is a student-designed system built to explore how plants grow in microgravity-like conditions. Our mission is to advance space biology by improving past nanolab designs while making the technology accessible and affordable for students, teachers, and hobbyists.

Why It Matters

Plants are essential for long-term space travel, providing food, oxygen, and psychological benefits for astronauts. However, microgravity affects plant growth, root orientation, and nutrient uptake in ways that are difficult to study outside professional research labs. Most students only learn about these challenges through textbooks or videos. Auxora Nanolabs bridges that gap by allowing users to observe, experiment, and analyze plant behavior in a simulated microgravity environment—making space science tangible, engaging, and approachable.

How It Works

The Auxora Nanolab creates a controlled environment that simulates microgravity conditions on Earth. Using custom software and hardware, users can adjust environmental factors like light intensity, water delivery, and airflow to observe how these variables affect plant growth.

  • Plant Growth Chamber: Provides a sealed environment for controlled experiments
  • Automated Systems: Precise control over water, light, and air circulation
  • Data Collection: Sensors monitor environmental conditions and plant health
  • User Interface: Intuitive software for experiment setup and monitoring

The Problem

There is currently no widely available, user-friendly system that allows students and educators to observe how plants behave under microgravity conditions. Most existing tools are too complex, expensive, or unavailable outside of research labs.

Project Objectives

  • Make space biology accessible to students, teachers, and hobbyists.
  • Improve reliability, usability, and educational value compared to earlier nanolab designs.
  • Create an affordable, ready-to-use system that simulates microgravity plant experiments.

Success Metrics

  • Functionality: Plants can successfully grow in a controlled chamber.
  • Reliability: LEDs, fans, pumps, sensors, and software work consistently.
  • Ease of Use: Anyone can set up and operate the system with minimal guidance.
  • Innovation: Improves accessibility and design over past projects.
  • Educational Value: Teachers and students find it engaging and helpful.
  • Affordability: Built from low-cost, widely available materials.
